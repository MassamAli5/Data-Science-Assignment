## How to read the `available_at` column

`intake` — the value exists at the moment the report is filed, so it is admissible for a
forward-looking claim. `POST-OUTCOME` — the value is written *after* the outcome is known.
Using any `POST-OUTCOME` column as a model feature leaks the answer (§8 of the notebook).

## Column notes, caveats and traps

### `agency`

*Publisher description:* Acronym of responding City Government Agency
*Analyst note:* Constant `DOT` **because of the cohort filter** — zero variance, no information.

### `borough`

*Publisher description:* Provided by the submitter and confirmed by geovalidation.
*Analyst note:* Includes `Unspecified` (125 rows) — excluded from borough comparisons.

### `closed_date`

*Publisher description:* Date SR was closed by responding agency
*Analyst note:* Null ⟺ request not closed (right-censored). 13 records have `closed_date < created_date`.

### `community_board`

*Publisher description:* Provided by geovalidation.
*Analyst note:* 100% non-null (administrative geocode always succeeds) but is **not** a street-level locator; 76 distinct values.

### `complaint_type`

*Publisher description:* This is the first level of a hierarchy identifying the topic of the incident or condition. Problem (formerly Complaint Type) broadly describes the topic of the incident or condition and are defined by the responding agencies. New Problems may be added in response to changes in customer demand.
*Analyst note:* Constant `Street Condition` by construction — zero variance.

### `created_date`

*Publisher description:* Date SR was created
*Analyst note:* Cohort filter field. Parsed as ISO-8601 **with milliseconds**; a naive `format="%Y-%m-%dT%H:%M:%S"` silently coerces every row to NaN.

### `descriptor`

*Publisher description:* This is associated to the Problem (formerly Complaint Type), and provides further detail on the incident or condition. Problem Detail (formerly Descriptor) values are dependent on the Problem, and are not always required in the service request.
*Analyst note:* Constant `Pothole` by construction — zero variance.

### `incident_address`

*Publisher description:* House number of incident address provided by submitter.
*Analyst note:* **Street-level, not point-level** — no house number. `"BROADWAY"` occurs 211× in this cohort, so it cannot identify a defect.

### `incident_zip`

*Publisher description:* Incident location zip code, provided by geo validation.
*Analyst note:* 2.21% null. 205 distinct values (includes non-NYC-adjacent codes).

### `open_data_channel_type`

*Publisher description:* Indicates how the SR was submitted to 311. i.e. By Phone, Online, Mobile, Other or Unknown.
*Analyst note:* Constant `UNKNOWN` for 100% of rows — **no information at all**, and it is the field you would need to study how requests are submitted.

### `resolution_action_updated_date`

*Publisher description:* Date when responding agency last updated the SR.
*Analyst note:* **POST-OUTCOME** — last back-office write

### `resolution_description`

*Publisher description:* Describes the last action taken on the SR by the responding agency. May describe next or future steps.
*Analyst note:* 13 distinct strings; mapped to 8 semantic classes in §9.1 of the notebook. This column **is** the outcome definition.

### `status`

*Publisher description:* Status of SR submitted
*Analyst note:* Only 2 values. `Closed` is NOT equivalent to `repaired` — see §9.1.

### `unique_key`

*Publisher description:* Unique identifier of a Service Request (SR) in the open data set
*Analyst note:* Primary key of the cohort. Verified unique (0 duplicates).

### `latitude`

*Publisher description:* Geo based Lat of the incident location
*Analyst note:* 52.52% coverage. Published as a number but arrives as a string for present rows.

### `location`

*Publisher description:* Combination of the geo based lat & long of the incident location
*Analyst note:* **Nested GeoJSON object**, not a scalar. Breaks `nunique()`, `groupby()`, and any blanket column sweep. Must be flattened or excluded.

### `longitude`

*Publisher description:* Geo based Long of the incident location
*Analyst note:* 52.52% coverage. Same string-typing quirk as `latitude`.
